🖨️ Production Specs

  • Trim: 8×10" portrait
  • Pages: 60 interior (30 spreads)
  • Cover: Soft cover, full color
  • Interior: Full color
  • Paper: Standard photo book paper (not premium lustre)
  • Binding: Perfect bound
  • Target budget: ~$700 total

Two paths to hit $700:

Option A — Fewer copies, full quality (recommended)

  • 25–30 copies
  • Premium color ink
  • ~$23–28 per copy
  • Pros: Design integrity preserved, photo quality holds up
  • Cons: Limited distribution — camp leadership picks who gets one

Option B — More copies, standard color

  • 45–55 copies
  • Standard color ink (less ink density)
  • ~$13–16 per copy
  • Pros: Covers most or all campers
  • Cons: Photos noticeably less vibrant, especially full-bleeds

Printer:

  • Lulu (primary) — best balance of price and photo book quality at this scale
  • Verify exact pricing with Lulu's Book Cost Calculator before committing

PDF deliverable:

  • 32 PDF pages total (1 front cover + 30 spreads + 1 back cover)
  • Front and back cover as singles, interior as 2-up spreads

Logistics:

  • Pay upfront (~$700)
  • Ships to one address — redistribute at camp in July
  • If Option A: decide distribution method before ordering (leadership picks, first-come, raffle, etc.)

🎯 To-dos

Decisions first

  • Choose Option A vs B (impacts everything downstream)
  • If Option A, decide how copies get distributed at camp
  • Confirm budget firm at ~$700

Production setup

  • Run exact quote in Lulu Book Cost Calculator with chosen spec
  • Order proof copy (~$25–30, included in total budget)
  • Lock printer and timeline

Content gathering

  • Collect camper questionnaire responses (ongoing)
  • Conduct Trout Creek Lodge owner interview, synthesize into 2–4 paragraphs
  • Pick 3 past year spotlights (founding year, pivotal middle year, most recent)
  • Gather hero photos and reflections for each spotlight
  • Draft iCamp 2026 theme intro copy
  • Draft opening letter
  • Draft closing reflection
  • Select 2–3 free-write submissions for the feature spread

Design

  • Finalize design system (typography, color, grid)
  • Build Figma master template library
  • Lay out all 30 interior spreads
  • Design front and back cover
  • Get camp leadership review on draft

Print

  • Export final print-ready PDF
  • Review proof, make corrections, re-upload
  • Place final order (Option A or B quantity)
  • Bring to camp in July
